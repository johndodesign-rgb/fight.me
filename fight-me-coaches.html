The Case for fight.me

One argument.
Three ways it matters.

You've seen it a hundred times. Adult converts who skip foundation work, plateau early, and blame the sport. fight.me addresses it before you have to.

01
It Won't Embarrass You In Front of Your Fighters
fight.me was built with the sport in mind — not around it. The progression logic, the terminology, the drill structure — all of it reflects how coaches actually think about fighter development. We're not putting gloves on a generic fitness app.
02
It Makes Your Job Easier, Not More Complicated
Your fighters show up more prepared. The questions they ask you are better questions. The foundation work happens between sessions — not instead of them. You get a coach dashboard that flags who needs attention before you notice it yourself.
03
Your Fighters Will Actually Stick With It
The system doesn't shame people for where they are. It reframes Foundation as the serious track — the one that separates fighters who last from fighters who plateau. That's a message your students will hear from the app so you don't have to repeat it.
System Overview

How it works
in your gym

Five moments that matter. Click each to see what it looks like.

1
Fighter takes the honest assessment
Six questions. No flattery. Covers conditioning, movement quality, experience, and recovery — the markers that actually predict readiness. The system places them. If they lie, the training reveals it.
2
They're placed in the right track
Foundation, Transition, or Fighter. Each placement comes with a rationale — not just a label. The app explains why they're there and what they're building toward. No one feels demoted.
3
You see it all from your dashboard
Every fighter's track, their current week, their performance signals. Flag colors tell you who needs attention before they bring it to you. Gym owner and private coach views both available.
4
The system self-corrects in real time
Performance signals — gassing out, hand drop, slow recovery — trigger targeted recommendations. The app routes fighters to fit.me for foundation work when data shows a gap. No confrontation needed.
5
They come back better
When a fighter completes a fit.me program, they return to fight.me with a real upgrade. The unlock moment is visible to both fighter and coach. Progress feels earned because it is.
