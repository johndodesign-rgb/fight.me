The Framework
HOW THIS WORKS

Four training days. Six movement patterns. Progressive overload across three phases. Cardio that doesn't require a treadmill. Every phase builds on the last — you're not starting over, you're loading the same foundation harder.

Core Principles

The 6 Patterns

Push · Pull · Squat · Hinge · Carry · Rotate. Hit all six every week. This is non-negotiable — everything else is variable.

Volume Target

10–15 working sets per pattern per week above 70% effort. Only 2–4 of those go truly hard (80%+). The rest build tissue.

Unilateral Work

One movement per pattern per week is single-limb. Fixes imbalances, builds rotational stability — boxing-critical.

Isolation Fills Gaps

2–3 sets per muscle group weekly to failure. Short rest (30–60 sec). Compounds alone leave development on the table.

Zone 2 Cardio

Jump rope or footwork at conversational pace — you can hold full sentences. Nose breathing only. Builds your engine.

Heart Rate at 46

Zone 2 ceiling: ~134 bpm (180 − 46). Feels embarrassingly easy. That's correct. Trust the adaptation.

Zone 2 via Jump Rope — The Ground Rules

Jump rope for zone 2 works, but you have to keep the intensity genuinely low. Slow single-bounce rhythm, not speed work. If you can't hold a conversation or nose-breathe continuously, slow down. Start with 10-minute blocks with 2-min rest. Work toward 20–30 continuous minutes by week 8. Footwork drills (shuffle, lateral step, pivot sequences) are excellent — they train boxing-specific movement patterns while staying aerobic.

Weeks 1–4
FOUNDATION

Build the pattern. Connective tissue adapts slower than muscle — don't skip this. Tempo work dominates: 3 seconds down, 1-second pause, normal up. Loads should feel moderate, never grinding. Zone 2 cardio only. Your aerobic base is being built here even if it doesn't feel like it.

Phase 1 Cardio — Tuesday Zone 2

20–25 minutes total. Jump rope: slow single-bounce, aiming for continuous movement. If you break rhythm, rest 90 seconds, continue. Alternatively: footwork ladder drills at low intensity, or simple shuffle/pivot sequences. Heart rate target: stay under 134 bpm. Should feel genuinely easy. Shadow boxing cool-down for 5 minutes — loose, no intensity, just movement.

Weeks 5–8
LOAD

Patterns are dialed. Now you load them. Increase working weight 5–10% across the board. Introduce variable resistance (bands on DB movements). HIIT enters on Saturday. Zone 2 sessions extend to 30 minutes. This is where you start to feel genuinely different.

Phase 2 of 3
Key Changes from Phase 1

Load Up

Push intensity to 75–80% on primary lifts. If phase 1 was 30lb DBs, try 35–40. Last 2 reps should be genuinely hard.

Variable Resistance

Add band to DB bench press and RDL. Loop red or black band under bench/feet. Movement gets harder at lockout — trains power.

HIIT Enters

Saturday: 4 rounds of 30-second jump rope all-out, 90-second rest. Keep it after lifting. Total: 8 minutes of real work.

Zone 2 Extends

Tuesday sessions hit 30 minutes continuous. Start mixing jump rope and footwork sequences in the same session.

Weeks 9–12
PEAK

This is what you built toward. True hard sets — 2–4 per session pushing 80%+. Full conditioning protocol. Jump rope intervals extend. You're training like an athlete now, not just exercising. Week 12 is a deload: same movements, 60% load, half the volume. Let the adaptation complete.

Phase 3 of 3
Key Changes from Phase 2

True Hard Sets

2–4 sets per session at 80%+ — last rep genuinely difficult. Not failure, but close. These are your strength drivers.

Speed Intervals Enter

Tuesday: 6× 15-second max-effort jump rope, 60-second full rest. Pure explosiveness. These train punch power transfer.

HIIT Extends

Saturday: 5–6 rounds, 45-second intervals, 90-second rest. You've earned the conditioning to handle this now.

Week 12: Deload

Drop to 60% load, cut sets by half. Keep moving, don't push. This is when gains actually solidify. Don't skip it.

Week 12 Deload Protocol

Use the same movements from weeks 9–11. Drop all weights to 60%. Cut sets by half — if you were doing 4×8, do 2×8. No HIIT. Zone 2 cardio only, 20 minutes max. This feels wrong. Do it anyway. Adaptation happens during recovery, not during the work. Come out of deload ready to test your numbers.
